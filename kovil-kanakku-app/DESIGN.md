## Brand & Style

The design system merges sacred Dravidian temple architecture with modern fintech precision. It establishes institutional credibility, spiritual reverence, and flawless fiscal transparency for temple trustees, donors, and administrative treasurers.

### Design Movement
**Modern Editorial Sacred** — A structured synthesis of tactile manuscript layouts and clean mobile accounting ergonomics. It replaces ornate, noisy festival clutter with disciplined editorial rhythm: deliberate ochre and crimson anchoring, balanced warm parchment space, clean gold filigree dividers, and high-legibility bilingual typographic structures.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
- **Audience:** Temple management committees (Devasthanam/Trusts), hereditary accountants, devotees, and diaspora patrons sponsoring poojas and temple infrastructure.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute fiscal trust (நம்பகத்தன்மை), devotion without distraction (பக்தி & அமைதி), and clarity in audit trails (தெளிவான கணக்கு).

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical South Indian temple iconography: Kumkumam (auspicious red/crimson), Gopura gold and brass vilakku (warm deep gold/ochre), Tulasi leaf (audit surplus/income green), and pure temple parchment (sacred off-white).

### Semantic & Ledger Color Tokens
- **Primary (`#7A1C1C` - Kumkum Crimson):** Applied to primary actions, key transaction headers, app bar identity, and expense debits.
- **Secondary (`#C88E2D` - Deep Temple Brass/Gold):** Denotes highlights, active tabs, donation tiers, auspicious certificates, and seal outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#1E5E3A` - Tulasi Ledger Green):** Used exclusively for income, credit donations, balance surplus, and successful audit verifications.
- **Neutral Background (`#F9F6F0` - Warm Parchment):** Base application background, providing eye comfort during prolonged bookkeeping sessions.
- **Surface Crisp Card (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast clean white for transaction entry panels, financial summary tiles, and receipt print views.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#1F1A17` - Karungali Charcoal):** Ultra-high legibility body text for ledger figures and bilingual labels.
- **Muted Border (`#E5DCB8` - Sandalwood Dust):** Structural grid borders and fine column dividers.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes bilingual optical balance across English script and Tamil glyphs (தமிழ் எழுத்துருக்கள்).

- **Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers classical editorial authority reminiscent of stone and copper inscriptions (கல்வெட்டு), suited for daily balance displays, temple trust designations, and formal receipt titles.
- **Body & Numerical Labels (`Noto Sans`):** Selected for its uncompromised clarity at compact data densities, preserving legibility across tabular transaction feeds, donor phone numbers, and GPay/UPI UTR numbers.
- **Numeral Formatting:** Monetary figures use tabular alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain strict vertical scanning of Rupee figures (`₹`).

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 4-column mobile grid built on an 8px architectural cadence.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Margins:** Standard canvas margin is 16px (`1rem`) on hand-held devices, expanding to 24px (`1.5rem`) on tablets.
- **Columns & Gutters:** 4 columns on mobile viewports with 16px gutters; 8 columns on tablet displays.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Form elements adhere strictly to 8px intervals. Compact ledger tables use 8px cell padding, while donation certificate previews and top summaries employ 16px to 24px internal padding.
- **Touch Targets:** All transactional touch targets (e.g., Quick Add buttons, donor call buttons, print voucher icons) adhere to an absolute minimum of 48px height.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp layered paper surfaces and fine metal trims rather than heavy artificial drop shadows.

- **Background Canvas:** Flat Warm Parchment (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Flat `#FFFFFF` container with a 1px solid sandalwood dust perimeter (`#E5DCB8`). Shadows are restrained to a warm ambient tint: `0 2px 8px rgba(122, 28, 28, 0.05)`.
- **Active Selection & Modals:** Elevated containers use `0 8px 24px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.08)` bordered with an accent gold top-line (2px `#C88E2D`).
- **Divider Accents:** Single-pixel hairline dividers (`#EFE8D3`), occasionally accented with a centered geometric diamond or miniature temple bell glyph in gold.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses subtle, soft corners (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius) to reflect structural stone masonry and sacred palm-leaf records (ஓலைச்சுவடி) without feeling clinical or overly playful.

- **Standard Cards & Ledger Containers:** 4px to 8px corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Action Buttons & Chips:** 4px corner radius for firm structural integrity.
- **Status Badges & Trust Marks:** 4px radius with thin 1px contrasting borders. Round avatars are reserved only for deity iconography and official trust seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (கட்டணம் / வரவு சேர்க்க):** Solid Crimson (`#7A1C1C`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, 4px border radius, 48px minimum height. On press, darkens to `#5E1515`.
- **Secondary (ரசீது அச்சிடுக / Print Receipt):** Sandalwood surface (`#F4EEDF`), Kumkum Crimson outline (1px `#7A1C1C`), and Crimson typography.
- **Gold Accent (பங்களிப்பாளர் / Special Pooja Tier):** Deep Gold (`#C88E2D`) with `#FFFFFF` or deep charcoal typography for premium endowments.

### Badges & Ledger Indicators
- **Income Badge (வரவு):** Background `#EAF5EE`, border 1px `#B2DEC3`, text `#1E5E3A` (`+ ₹1,008`).
- **Expense Badge (செலவு):** Background `#FDF0F0`, border 1px `#F2BEBE`, text `#7A1C1C` (`- ₹500`).
- **Audit / Verified Seal (தணிக்கை செய்யப்பட்டது):** Soft gold background (`#FAF3E0`), 1px `#C88E2D` border, pairing a checkmark with a traditional Kalasam or lotus glyph.

### Input Fields & Search
- Form fields feature an off-white fill (`#FFFFFF`), an outer border in `#D4C9A8`, and 8px padding.
- Focused fields shift border color to `#7A1C1C` with a 1px inner glow.
- Numeric inputs for donation entries (ரூபாய்) include a persistent, bold prefix (`₹`) formatted in `Noto Serif`.

### Cards & Transaction Rows
- **Summary Tile:** High-level dashboard cards showing Total Balance (மொத்த இருப்பு), Day Income, and Day Expense, bounded by `#E5DCB8` with a 3px top border in `#7A1C1C` or `#C88E2D`.
- **Ledger List Item:** Clean split row with donor/pooja name and head of account on the left, date/time in secondary charcoal, and amount aligned right with its corresponding income green or expense crimson badge.
- **Digital Receipt Voucher:** Styled with a decorative scalloped or serrated boundary edge, containing bilingual donor fields, temple registration number, and signed trustee signoff box.
